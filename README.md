**An updated and improved addon based on "[Wider Professions - Better Window and Filters](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wider-professions)" by [zeratoruspatokus](https://www.curseforge.com/members/zeratoruspatokus/projects)**

# Timber's Wider Professions

Add a whole lot of functionality to your profession/skill windows. It starts with making them double-wide, and continues with features like unlocking the window to allow you to drag it around your screen, adding the level brackets (the orange/yellow/green/gray numbers) to the skill list, and more. Not only does this addon improve the skill windows, but it keeps things looking as close to vanilla as possible while doing so.

# Features

### Larger profession/skill window

The new window is twice as wide as the standard window. This allows us to split the window into two distinct panels: a scrollable list of skills, and a details panel. By splitting these two up, we can view a lot more of the skill list at once, and we can also add a bunch of information to that skill list to help us along the way.

Want to see even more of the skill list? Now it's even easier! Increase the height of the window to whatever feels comfortable to you. Go to the addon's options page to set it.

### Favorite recipes

Do you have some profession items that you use frequently, but hate scrolling to find? Click the skill, then the star icon in the details panel, and the item will show up at the top of the list in a new "Favorites" category. Never scroll again to create those things you use most often.

### Search bar and filtering

Find specific skill items faster than ever with filtering. Search for an item, a crafting material to see its uses, or only view skill items that will give you levels; there are so many ways to look at the data.

### Improved categories

Certain skills have a very bare-bones approach in the default profession/skill window, and those default categories are not always... intuitive. Let us fix that for you! Alchemy, cooking, enchanting, and beast training all have the option (user-togglable) to display categories for filtering that actually make sense. Want to go back to the original categories? Easy! Simply go into the options and uncheck the skills you want to return to "factory" settings.

### Skill highlights based on level-up likelihood OR item rarity

The standard view is the orange/yellow/green/gray highlights to show how likely you are to get a level-up from a skill item. Now, however, we've added a second option: item rarity coloring. See the epic and rare recipes you have even easier than ever. The choice is yours!

### Find new recipes in a snap with special highlights

Ever learn a new recipe, but then struggle to find where it went in your skill list? Struggle no longer! When you learn a new recipe (or load the addon for the first time), you'll find a special highlight box around the item. Spot your new recipes fast and easy, and stop wasting time looking through your skill list.

### Support for Hunters and Rogues, with pet training and poison skill windows

Not just for professions, this new, wider window brings the fun to every skill!

### Localization

Similar to the original addon, this addon features localization in multiple languages: English, Spanish (EU or MX), German, and French. More to come soon!

### WoW: Forever

On WoW: Forever the addon replaces the crafting page of the new profession window with its own: the same window frame and painted art, but with the skill-level brackets on every recipe, three ways to list recipes (Blizzard's categories, one list by skill level, one list alphabetical) with Favorites always on top, a search box that also matches reagents, adjustable height, and an enchant target slot so you can enchant your own gear without opening your bags. Profession tabs sit on the right edge; the Skills tab shows Blizzard's overview page. It also works around a Forever bug where opening one profession sometimes opened another.
